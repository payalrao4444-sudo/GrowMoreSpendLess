♻️ Waste to Best 🌿
Turn kitchen waste into powerful organic fertilizer
🍂 Compost at Home
  • Vegetable peels & fruit scraps
  • Coffee grounds & tea leaves
  • Dry leaves & newspaper
  • Eggshells for calcium
  • Ready in 4–8 weeks
🪱 Vermicompost
  • Use red wigglers (earthworms)
  • Feed kitchen scraps daily
  • Ready castings in 60–90 days
  • Highest nutrient density fertilizer
  • Improves soil structure
🌿 Neem Oil Spray
  • 5ml neem oil + 1L water
  • Add 2 drops dish soap to emulsify
  • Spray weekly in evening
  • Controls 200+ pests
  • Safe for beneficial insects
🐄 Cow Dung Slurry
  • Mix 1 part cow dung : 5 parts water
  • Let ferment for 1–2 days
  • Dilute before applying to roots
  • Rich in nitrogen & microbes
  • Excellent soil conditioner
🍌 Banana Peel Fertilizer
  • Soak peels in water 48 hrs
  • Strain and use as liquid feed
  • High in potassium & phosphorus
  • Promotes flowering & fruiting
  • Free from kitchen waste
🧄 Garlic Pest Spray
  • Blend 10 garlic cloves + 1L water
  • Strain through cloth
  • Spray on affected leaves
  • Repels aphids, caterpillars
  • Apply every 7 days

📈 Growth Stage Guidance
Step-by-step care for each growth phase
🌰
Seed
Sow seeds 1–2cm deep. Keep moist but not waterlogged. Temperature 20–25°C ideal. Use sterilized seed-starting mix.
🌱
Germination
Tiny shoots emerge in 5–14 days. Provide 12–16 hrs indirect light. Keep soil evenly moist. Thin seedlings if crowded.
🌿
Growth
Apply diluted compost tea weekly. Ensure 6+ hrs sunlight. Water deeply but less often. Watch for pests.
🌸
Flowering
Reduce nitrogen, increase phosphorus (banana peel). Hand-pollinate if needed. Avoid over-watering. Remove dead flowers.
🍅
Fruiting
Support heavy fruits. Water consistently. Harvest when ripe to encourage more production. Apply potassium-rich fertilizer.
